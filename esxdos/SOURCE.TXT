esxDOS 0.8.9 for the DivMMC, by Papaya Dezign (http://www.esxdos.org).

These files come unchanged from http://www.esxdos.org/files/esxdos089.zip
(the DivIDE and MB03+ versions and the .TAP installers are left out). They
are the property of their authors and are included for convenience, as they
are freely available from esxdos.org.

  ESXMMC.BIN       the DivMMC firmware (8K EEPROM image) used by ochenta
  SYS/ BIN/ TMP/   the files that go on the SD card; ochenta copies them onto
                   a new card with Disk > SD card > New card with ESXDOS
  README.TXT, ESX089.NFO, DOCS/   esxDOS documentation
