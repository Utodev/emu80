# ROMs

| File | Machine |
|---|---|
| `48.rom` | 48K |
| `128-0.rom`, `128-1.rom` | 128K |
| `plus2-0.rom`, `plus2-1.rom` | +2 |
| `plus3-0.rom` … `plus3-3.rom` | +2B and +3 |

These ROMs are copyright Amstrad, who allow distribution of the ROMs but
retain the copyright. You may not sell the ROMs or embed the ROMs in
hardware, although it is allowed to sell a product which contains the ROMs,
so long as the charge is being made for the product, not for the ROMs
themselves.

The files were taken from the FUSE emulator distribution.

`currah.rom` is the 2K ROM of the Currah µSpeech (© Currah 1983) and
`sp0256-al2.rom` the 2K mask ROM of its speech chip, the General Instrument
SP0256-AL2. Microchip Technology holds the copyright to the SP0256-AL2 ROM
image and the intellectual property rights to the algorithms and data it
contains; Microchip allowed Joe Zbiciak to distribute it as long as this is
acknowledged (spatula-city.org/~im14u2c/sp0256-al2/). Both files are from
k1.spdns.de (Vintage/Sinclair/82/Peripherals/Currah uSpeech).

`tbblue_loader.rom` is the boot ROM of the TBBlue board of the ZX Spectrum
Next (© Victor Trucco and Fabio Belavenuto, GPL v3), taken from the ZEsarUX
emulator distribution. It loads the firmware (`TBBLUE.FW`) from the SD card;
the firmware and everything else of the Next come from its official
distribution, which is not included (see the `specnext` folder).

`48.sym` lists the 48K ROM routines and system variables with the names used
in *The Complete Spectrum ROM Disassembly* (Ian Logan and Frank O'Hara), taken
from Richard Dymond's SkoolKit disassembly (github.com/skoolkid/rom, GPL v3).

`128-0.sym`, `plus2-0.sym` and `plus3-*.sym` are generated by
`tools/make-rom-symbols.py` from the disassemblies in
github.com/ZXSpectrumVault/rom-disassemblies (Paul Farrow, Garry Lancaster and
others); every label was checked against these ROM files.

`cpc464.rom`, `cpc664.rom` and `cpc6128.rom` (32K each: the 16K operating
system followed by the 16K Locomotive BASIC of the model) and `amsdos.rom`
(the 16K AMSDOS disc ROM of the CPC 664 and 6128) are the firmware of the
Amstrad CPC, © Amstrad. Amstrad allows the distribution of the CPC ROMs
for non-commercial use, retaining the copyright. The files were taken from
the Caprice32 emulator distribution (the same images as WinAPE's `OS464.ROM`
+ `BASIC1-0.ROM`, `OS664.ROM` + `BASIC664.ROM`, `OS6128.ROM` + `BASIC1-1.ROM`
and `AMSDOS.ROM`).
